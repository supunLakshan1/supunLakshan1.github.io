Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the "video creator" app (the "App") handles information. The App is a private tool operated by the owner of the Mr. Mind channel (the "Owner") to publish the Owner's own videos.

Who uses the App

Only the Owner uses the App. It is not available to the public, and it does not let anyone else sign in.

Information the App accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with the Owner's permission, the App uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the Owner's own channel and to read that channel's basic details (such as its name) to confirm the right channel is used.
  • TikTok and Facebook: with the Owner's permission, the App uses the TikTok Content Posting API and the Facebook Graph API to publish videos to the Owner's own TikTok account and Facebook Page.
  • Access tokens: the sign-in tokens these services return are stored only on the Owner's own computer and are used only to publish the Owner's videos.

Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, store or process personal information about viewers or any other users. It does not read comments, messages, contacts or viewing history, and it uses no cookies or tracking.

Sharing

No information is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platforms named above, as chosen by the Owner.

Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The Owner can revoke the App's access at any time from their Google account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and from the app settings of TikTok and Facebook. Stored tokens are then deleted from the Owner's computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Mr. Mind YouTube channel.
