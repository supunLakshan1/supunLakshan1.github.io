Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The "video creator" app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Mr. Mind channel. It does not collect or store personal data about other people. The only data it keeps is the owner's own sign-in tokens for YouTube, TikTok and Facebook, stored on the owner's computer.

How to remove the app's access and delete its data

  1. Facebook: go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), select video creator and click Remove.
  2. Google / YouTube: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select video creator and choose Remove access.
  3. TikTok: in the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, select video creator and remove it.

When access is removed, the saved tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computer. No other copies exist.

Request deletion

If you believe the app holds any data about you, send a request through the Mr. Mind YouTube channel and it will be deleted within 30 days.
